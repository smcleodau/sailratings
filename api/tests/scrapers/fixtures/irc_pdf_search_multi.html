Valid Boat Data

All valid IRC boat data is available in digital format from the search below to enable sailors and race organisers to check boat configurations.

Please enter certificate number, boat name or sail number to search for a boat and download a PDF file of the boat’s data and drawing.

50614_KARAKOA - SEC_PHI8088.pdf Download

The accuracy of the measurements supplied shall be the owner’s responsibility. The boat drawing is approximated based on IRC rated data.

Published data shown may be for an expired or invalidated certificate. The Valid Rating Listing is the definitive source for valid ratings.

Only certificates issued after 1st January 2024 are available. The data is updated daily so will be available 24 hours after the issue of the certificate.

If you wish to purchase a full copy certificate for a boat, please read the policy and contact your IRC Rule Authority.
